# Limits confirmed from saved results

- **No learned coast benefit from the supplied properties.** Oracle was worse than Plain on the revised moving-coast gate; this does not show that friction is uninformative, because the explicit friction formula closely matched simulated deceleration and mass versus friction were not isolated in the Oracle input. [Coast gate](/home/ubuntu/faliure/clean/shove_gate/revised_velocity/RESULTS.md:3), [physics check](/home/ubuntu/faliure/clean/shove_gate/revised_velocity/RESULTS.md:19), [joint inputs](/home/ubuntu/faliure/clean/shove_gate/adaptation_compare/RESULTS.md:18).
- **Push-focused belief fitting worsened other predictions.** Step 2 fitted belief had worse moving-coast and full-state errors than Plain; the revised fitting objective and stronger pull toward average belief did not establish improvement across these scores, and the changes do not isolate the penalty's causal effect. [Step 2 secondary scores](/home/ubuntu/faliure/clean/shove_gate/push_latent/RESULTS.md:150), [coast scores](/home/ubuntu/faliure/clean/shove_gate/push_latent/RESULTS.md:174), [revised settings](/home/ubuntu/faliure/clean/shove_gate/adaptation_compare/VALIDATION_SELECTION.md:3), [Step 3 comparison](/home/ubuntu/faliure/clean/shove_gate/adaptation_compare/RESULTS.md:9).
- **One-step gains did not establish reliable longer forecasts.** Fitted belief worsened recursive rollout and stopping-position errors; successive predictions can accumulate errors even when immediate push prediction improves. [Saved rollout and stopping scores](/home/ubuntu/faliure/clean/shove_gate/push_latent/RESULTS.md:156).
- **The shove task uses idealized simulation.** Force is applied directly at the cube's center of mass with exact simulator state and a fixed floor material, bypassing robot contact and sensing; real-arm results were **not found**. [Generator and force protocol](/home/ubuntu/faliure/clean/shove_gate/push_latent/PROTOCOL.md:22), [simulation scope](/home/ubuntu/faliure/clean/shove_gate/RESULTS_DIRECT.md:95), [runtime scope](/home/ubuntu/faliure/robot_adaptation/runtime/README.md:37).
