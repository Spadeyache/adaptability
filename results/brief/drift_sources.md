# Drift figure: saved-data provenance and interpretation

`fig_drift.png` and `fig_drift.svg` are generated by `drift_figure.py`, which imports no experiment, predictor, optimizer, model, checkpoint, or simulator code. Run from the repository root with `.venv/bin/python results/brief/drift_figure.py`. All reads are saved score CSVs/JSONs; `drift_source_hashes.json` records their SHA-256 values. `drift_numbers.csv` contains every plotted aggregate, its individual seed values, and source file plus CSV lines or JSON key.

- **New-cube lines:** At visit *n*, independently average each seed's current-cube push velocity-change scores for the first *n* visited cubes, then plot the mean and sample standard deviation across seeds. Source: `clean/shove_gate/adaptation_compare/results/current_cube_sequence_scores.csv`, rows where `method=B` and `metric=push_velocity_nmse`; seeds 101, 211, 307, 401, 503. This is the cumulative mean of observations made immediately after each cube's adaptation. It is **not** an evaluation of the final network on all visited cubes. The same-cube Plain comparator uses `results/baselines/plain_seed{seed}.json → query.aggregates.push_velocity_nmse.per_cube` in the same order.
- **Independent-adaptation diamonds:** These are means over all 30 test cubes when the original Plain network is restored before adapting to each cube on five support shoves. They are not observations at a later visit, nor the score on the designated first cube. Source: `results/prefix_scores.csv`, `method=B`, `support_shoves=5`, `metric=push_velocity_nmse`; the separate tick says “Reset per cube.” The Plain diamond averages the same 30 cubes.
- **Training-cube lines:** At each saved visit snapshot, evaluate held-out shoves 8–9 on weight-training cubes 0–179. Source: `results/sequence_scores.csv`, `method=B`, `population=training_validation`, `metric=composite`. Snapshot zero is saved and equals Plain. The line joins saved snapshots; it does not imply evaluation at unsaved visits. The frozen Plain comparator comes from `baselines/plain_seed{seed}.json → training_validation.scores.composite`.
- **Metric:** New-cube scores use push horizontal-velocity-change NMSE. Training-cube “combined prediction error” is the equal average of push velocity-change, moving-coast velocity-change, and full-state NMSE, each with its own fitting-data scale; this is not the same score as the left panel. NMSE means squared prediction error divided by a fixed training-data scale. All values are dimensionless; lower is better. Definition: `clean/shove_gate/adaptation_compare/RESULTS.md:7` and `VALIDATION_SELECTION.md:14`.
- **Uncertainty and scope:** Bands and diamond error bars are sample standard deviations across five training seeds, not confidence intervals over cubes or visiting orders. One fixed order, cube IDs 290–319. The results concern exactly five support shoves and query shoves 5–9. Definitions: `RESULTS.md:7`, `RESULTS.md:58`, `RESULTS.md:224`.

## Caption suggestion (two sentences)

When fine-tuning carries its network weights from cube to cube, the cumulative push-prediction error on new cubes and the error on held-out interactions from training cubes exceed the no-adaptation baseline, most clearly at the larger learning rate; diamonds show the separately measured result when weights are reset before each cube. The curves use one fixed visiting order and show variation across training seeds, so they describe this run rather than establishing general forgetting; the combined training-cube score averages separately scaled push, coast, and full-state errors.
