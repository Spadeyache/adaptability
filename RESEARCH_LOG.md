# Research log

## 2026-11-12 · Object beliefs in simulated PickCube

**Question.** Can a robot use prediction error to estimate a cube's physical properties, remember them when the cube returns, and use experience with other cubes to improve its first guess? The motion policy stays frozen throughout. A world model predicts robot and object motion from their current state and the commanded action. A belief is the robot's current estimate of an object's properties and its uncertainty.

**Work performed today.** Inspected installed code, checked saved rollout arrays and the original evaluation rules, rescored saved results, and assembled the advisor packet. ### What can be reused for the pilot?

The dense archive spans 12 mass levels and eight friction levels within the policy bounds. The existing in-range split has 17 training, seven validation and eight test settings. A held-out setting has no episodes used for fitting. The same 10 reset seeds recur across settings. At 500 g and friction 0.4, the 10 attempts all made contact and none met the archived lift criterion. [Condition-level inventory](results/stage0/lift_inventory.json)

We also checked whether the policy pushed cubes incidentally on approach. We required measured finger contact on at least three of the five simulator samples per control step, the cube bottom within 1 mm of the tabletop before and after each step, and at least two consecutive steps before the first grasp. Of 32 settings, 18 had a lift and at least 5 mm of horizontal motion during such a contact. At 10 mm this fell to 14 and at 20 mm it fell to five. Lift and lateral contact can occur in different attempts on the same setting. These distances were chosen during this exploratory audit. Selecting the easiest-to-move cubes would bias a comparison. [Exact rule and episode references](results/stage0/push_coverage.json)

For the settling check, accuracy meant within 40 g through the end of an attempt for at least ten observations. Unreached attempts contributed the full 100-step budget. Averaged across trials 2–5, the carried filter saved 44.1 observed steps under that rule (paired 95% interval 22.7–65.5, resampling the 12 sequences). The first measurement follows the first action. Estimates already accurate at that point have zero observed waiting steps. [Definition and trial-level counts](results/stage0/retention_check.json)

**Next decision.** Keep the 32 settings and their original splits for the archive analysis. Use the incidental contacts as exploratory evidence. To meet Stage 1 as written, add comparable deliberate pushes at preselected existing settings and verify measured contact. Retain the hard 500 g condition and any future failed lifts. The ten-cube pilot and full collection size await agreement and timing. For the main experiment, preselect the error threshold before seeing deployment outcomes and compare priors on the same cube orders and interactions, using only earlier cubes' estimates to update the next guess.
